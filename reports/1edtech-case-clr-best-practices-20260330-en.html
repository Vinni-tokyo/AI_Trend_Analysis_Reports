Key Terminology

CASE (Competency and Academic Standards Exchange) - An open technical standard by 1EdTech for exchanging learning standards, competencies, and learning outcomes in a machine-readable digital format. Uses JSON and REST APIs, assigning each competency a GUID.
CLR (Comprehensive Learner Record) - A standard for digitally recording a learner's entire educational journey. Covers formal academic achievement as well as co-curricular activities, skills, competencies, and career milestones.
Open Badges 3.0 - A digital badge standard adopting W3C Verifiable Credentials. Certifies learning achievements in visual, verifiable form and connects to competencies by embedding CASE GUIDs.
LER-RS (Learning and Employment Resume Standard) - A machine-readable resume standard jointly developed by HR Open Standards and 1EdTech. Serves as a bridge enabling Open Badges and CLR to be utilized in hiring systems.
GUID (Globally Unique Identifier) - A unique identifier assigned to each learning standard and competency in CASE frameworks, enabling precise cross-system referencing of identical competencies.
W3C Verifiable Credentials - A digital credential data standard defined by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C). Adopted by CLR 2.0 and Open Badges 3.0 to ensure tamper resistance and verifiability.
CTDL (Credential Transparency Description Language) - A credential transparency language developed by Credential Engine. Compatible with CLR, it structures detailed credential information (skills, requirements, costs, etc.) in a standardized format.
