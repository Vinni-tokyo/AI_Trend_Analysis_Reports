Key Terminology

LLM (Large Language Model) - A deep learning model trained on massive text corpora capable of generating human-quality text. GPT-4o (OpenAI) and Claude Opus (Anthropic) are leading examples used in education content generation
RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) - An architecture that combines LLM text generation with real-time retrieval from curated knowledge bases to improve factual accuracy and reduce hallucination in generated content
SCORM (Sharable Content Object Reference Model) - An international standard for e-learning content packaging that ensures interoperability across Learning Management Systems. SCORM-compliant content can be deployed on 95%+ of enterprise LMS platforms
xAPI (Experience API) - A next-generation learning data standard (successor to SCORM) that tracks a wider range of learning experiences including mobile, simulation, and informal learning activities
Knowledge Graph - A structured representation of domain knowledge as interconnected concepts, enabling AI systems to understand prerequisite relationships, skill dependencies, and optimal learning sequences
Multimodal AI - AI systems capable of processing and generating multiple content types (text, images, video, audio) within a unified model, enabling integrated content creation pipelines
Fine-Tuning - The process of further training a pre-trained LLM on domain-specific data (e.g., medical education, legal compliance) to improve generation quality and accuracy for specialized content areas
AI Avatar - A synthetic digital human generated by AI that can deliver video content with natural speech, lip movement, and gestures. Used in education to create instructor-led video without live recording
Bloom's Taxonomy - A hierarchical framework classifying cognitive learning objectives (Remember, Understand, Apply, Analyze, Evaluate, Create) widely used in instructional design and now encoded into AI prompt templates for educational content generation
Prompt Engineering - The practice of designing structured input prompts to guide LLM outputs toward desired formats, quality levels, and pedagogical standards in education content generation
