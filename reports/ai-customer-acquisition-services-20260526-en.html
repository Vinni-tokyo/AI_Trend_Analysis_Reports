Top 5 Trends

1. The Rise of Agentic Marketing

Status: In 2026, AI-driven customer acquisition has moved beyond using generative AI to speed up individual tasks. Marketers now delegate full work units—decision and execution—to AI agents, while humans focus on judgment and editing. Salesforce frames the shift as marketers becoming "strategic directors who orchestrate AI agents." Real implementations are visible across SoftBank logistics (40% delivery efficiency gain in Japan) and Hyundai Motors / telecom AICCs in Korea.

Core Capabilities

• Autonomous campaigns: Agents handle segmentation, content generation, distribution, and optimization end-to-end
• Lead scoring: Real-time prioritization based on behavioral data (HubSpot Breeze, Salesforce Einstein)
• Delegated decisions: Humans set guardrails; agents execute

Market Data

• AI customer service market: $15.12B in 2026 → $47.82B by 2030 (25.8% CAGR)
• Routine automation: 80% of interactions handled by AI in 2026 (Gartner)
• ROI: $3.50 returned per $1 invested in AI customer service
• Cost reduction: $80B in contact-center labor savings projected for 2026

2. Hyper-Personalization: The Starbucks DeepBrew Model Spreads

Status: Beyond simple recommendations, real-time 1-to-1 promotions combining purchase history, preference, location, time, and weather have become the norm. Starbucks's proprietary DeepBrew AI platform delivers hyper-personalized offers to tens of millions; Korean coffee chains and Japanese beauty / retail brands are rapidly replicating the pattern.

Japan Cases

• Salon group Lond: LINE mini-app "Saloriza" rollout; some stores see 40% of bookings via LINE
• Beauty Salon Be+ (Tsukuba): Acquired 250+ repeat customers via LINE auto-reply
• Restaurants: AI congestion-forecast calendars on websites distribute visit timing

Korea Cases

• Starbucks Korea: Mobile Order data + AI recommendations lift average ticket size
• KakaoTalk Channel: AlimTalk-based segment campaigns, integrated review management for Musinsa / Coupang
• Naver SmartStore: Tools like "Search Doogam" optimize product names and hashtags for higher visibility

3. The LLMO/AIO Era: ChatGPT Atlas, Perplexity, and Google AI Mode as New Traffic Channels

Status: AI browsers and AI search engines—ChatGPT Atlas, Perplexity Comet, Google AI Overview—have replaced large swaths of SEO traffic. The question shifted from "How do we get noticed?" to "How do we rank in the AI's recommendation order?" (LLMO: LLM Optimization). Japan already has 21 specialized LLMO agencies on the market.

Zero-Click Environment Shifts

• Traffic decline: SERP CTR falls; citation inside AI summaries becomes key
• Structured data: Schema.org, FAQ markup, and trust signals drive citation odds
• Brand mentions: Third-party media mentions weight AI recommendation rankings

LLMO Response Strategy

• Content redesign: Clear definitions, statistics, and citation-friendly formats
• Japan LLMO market: 2026 comparison guides list 21 specialized agencies
• Korea: Needs to address Naver Cue while optimizing for ChatGPT citations

4. Predictive Lead Scoring & ABM Become the Standard

Status: In B2B, ABM (Account-Based Marketing) using anonymous intent data from 6sense, Demandbase, Bombora, and Cognism has become the default for identifying in-market accounts. 6sense was named a Forrester Leader in B2B Revenue Marketing 2026, while HubSpot and Salesforce have embedded predictive scoring into their CRMs, lowering entry barriers.

Core Technology

• Intent data: Behavioral signals aggregated from review sites (G2, TrustRadius)
• Predictive scoring: Models trained on past won deals applied to new leads
• Buying-group insight: Mapping the whole buying committee, not a single contact

Adoption in Japan & Korea

• Japan: Marketo / HubSpot adoption led by SaaS companies; ICT Research projects 35.53M generative-AI users by end of 2026
• Korea: Kakao Enterprise and Samsung SDS B2B teams expanding Salesforce Einstein use
• Shared gap: Many mid-market firms in both countries still have weak first-party data

📊 Credibility Assessment:8.5/10

Weaknesses: Korea B2B adoption statistics rely on a small sample

Outlook: ABM is no longer the domain of global SaaS alone. By 2027, more than 30% of mid-market B2B firms in Japan and Korea will likely embed predictive scoring into their sales process.

Japan's wave centers on LINE-ecosystem solutions for beauty and restaurants (Saloriza, AI-BOUZ, CAT.AI); Korea's wave runs on Kakao / Naver-ecosystem e-commerce tooling (Search Doogam, ALF chatbot, Zentoo recommendations).

📖 Key Terminology

AI Customer Acquisition: Marketing activities that use AI to automate the full path from prospect discovery to conversion
Agentic Marketing: A marketing approach where AI agents autonomously design, execute, and optimize campaigns
LLMO (LLM Optimization): The discipline of optimizing content so it gets cited and recommended by LLM-based search systems like ChatGPT and Perplexity — the AI-era successor to SEO
ABM (Account-Based Marketing): A B2B strategy that aligns sales and marketing around target companies (accounts) rather than individual leads
Intent Data: Anonymous behavioral signals captured from prospects on third-party sites (reviews, searches, content)
Zero-Click Environment: A search behavior shift where AI answers directly, leaving users no need to visit external sites
