Key Terminology

AI Course Builder - A software platform that uses artificial intelligence to automatically generate complete online courses from input materials such as documents, slides, or topic descriptions. Outputs typically include structured lessons, quizzes, and SCORM-compliant packages.
SCORM (Sharable Content Object Reference Model) - A set of technical standards for eLearning content that ensures interoperability between learning content and Learning Management Systems. SCORM-compliant content can be uploaded to any SCORM-compatible LMS.
Generative AI Video - Video content produced by artificial intelligence models that synthesize realistic human avatars, speech, and motion from text inputs. Eliminates the need for cameras, studios, and human actors for training and educational content.
LMS (Learning Management System) - A software platform for delivering, tracking, and managing educational and training programs. Enterprise LMS platforms like Docebo and Cornerstone are increasingly embedding AI for content generation and personalized learning paths.
Scenario Branching - An interactive learning design technique where learners make choices that lead to different outcomes within a video or course. Used extensively in compliance and soft-skills training to simulate real-world decision-making.
MCP (Model Context Protocol) - An open protocol developed by Anthropic that enables AI assistants (such as Claude and ChatGPT) to directly access and integrate external data sources and tools. Udemy's MCP Server allows learning content to be surfaced within AI assistant conversations.
AI Curriculum Generator - An AI tool specifically designed for educators that automatically creates standards-aligned lesson plans, assessments, and teaching materials based on learning objectives and curriculum requirements.
