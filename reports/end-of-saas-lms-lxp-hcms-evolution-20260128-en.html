Key Terminology

SaaS (Software as a Service) - A cloud-based business model delivering software through subscriptions. Traditionally uses per-seat (user count) monthly or annual billing.
LMS (Learning Management System) - An admin-driven platform for delivering, tracking, and managing educational content. Primarily uses the SCORM standard.
LXP (Learning Experience Platform) - A next-generation learner-centric platform providing AI-based personalized recommendations, microlearning, and social learning. Uses the xAPI standard.
HCMS/HCM (Human Capital Management System) - A strategic HR management platform that goes beyond HRIS/HRMS to encompass talent development, succession planning, global HR, and AI analytics.
Agentic AI - AI technology that goes beyond user assistance to autonomously execute multi-step processes and make decisions. A key driver fundamentally transforming SaaS business models.
xAPI (Experience API) - A learning tracking standard used by LXPs that, unlike SCORM, can track all learning behaviors across devices and platforms and measure their correlation with on-the-job performance.
Usage-Based Pricing - A billing model based on actual usage, throughput, or outcomes rather than fixed seat counts. The emerging standard replacing traditional per-seat SaaS pricing in the AI era.
