1. Surging Impact Factor and Its Correlation with the AI Research Boom

Both metrics reflect a sustained upward trajectory driven by the explosive growth of AI and machine learning research. ESWA has firmly established itself as a primary publication venue in this domain (Source: Resurchify, BioxBio, 2024).

2. Deepening and Diversification of AI/ML Research Domains

Of the 1,385 articles published in 2024, AI/ML applications constitute the core content, with papers on healthcare, finance, and Industry 4.0 applications recording particularly high citation rates (Source: SciSpace 2024 article analysis).

3. Review Speed Concerns and Author Experience

Current Status: ESWA's average review time of approximately 13.8 months is notably long among AI/CS journals. According to SciRev and Academic Accelerator author reviews, some cases saw the first review return after 73.9 weeks (approximately 17 months). The rapid increase in manuscript submissions combined with reviewer scarcity are the primary drivers (Source: LetPub, SciRev, Academic Accelerator).

Review Process Characteristics

• Review type: Single-blind peer review
• Minimum reviewers: 2 independent experts
• Average review duration: ~13.8 months
• Longest reported case: 73.9 weeks (~17 months)
• Initial editor check: Within a few weeks of submission

Author Feedback & Pain Points

• Top complaint: Long wait times for review
• Communication: Slow editor responsiveness reported
• Review quality: High variability across reviewers
• Author tip: Proactively send status inquiry emails
• Desk rejection: Fast rejection for out-of-scope papers

Credibility Assessment: 7.5/10
Strengths: Grounded in real author experience data from LetPub and SciRev
Limitation: High individual variability limits statistical representativeness
🔗 Related Links: LetPub Review Speed | SciRev Author Reviews | Academic Accelerator

Outlook: Given the continued rise in submission volume, review delays are unlikely to resolve quickly. For time-sensitive research, Knowledge-Based Systems and Applied Intelligence offer practical alternatives.

5. Strengthened Rejection Policy and Scope Clarification

Current Status: ESWA has made its rejection criteria increasingly explicit. Papers that merely repackage existing concepts under new metaphors (e.g., repetitive bio-inspired optimization algorithm variants) or lack clear applied contribution are rejected at the desk review stage (Source: ESWA Author Guidelines).

Publication-Suitable Paper Types

• Application-driven: Real-world AI/ML problem solving
• Comparative experiments: Convincing superiority over baselines
• Novel systems: New expert system architectures
• Interdisciplinary: AI + domain expertise synergy
• Practical contribution: Demonstrated industrial/social applicability

Unsuitable Paper Types

• Metaphor algorithms: Mere repackaging of optimization
• Pure theory: Math-only without application
• Low novelty: Only incremental extension of prior work
• Small-scale experiments: Non-representative datasets
• Out-of-scope: Topics unrelated to ESWA's focus areas

Credibility Assessment: 8/10
Strengths: Based on ESWA's official author guidelines and editor statements
Limitation: Actual desk rejection rate statistics are not publicly disclosed

Outlook: As AI research quality standards rise broadly, ESWA's acceptance criteria are expected to tighten further. Carefully reviewing author guidelines before submission and clearly articulating applied contributions are key to acceptance success.

Key Terminology

Impact Factor (IF) - Published by Clarivate's Web of Science JCR; measures the average number of citations received per article in a given journal over the preceding two years
CiteScore - Elsevier's Scopus-based citation metric calculated over a 4-year window; typically higher than the IF
h-index - The maximum value h such that h papers have each been cited at least h times. ESWA's h-index of 290 means 290 papers have each accumulated 290+ citations
SJR (SCImago Journal Rank) - A weighted journal influence metric that accounts for the prestige of citing journals
Single-blind Review - Peer review in which reviewers know the authors' identities but authors do not know who the reviewers are
APC (Article Publishing Charge) - The fee paid by authors (or their institutions) to publish a paper open access
Q1 (Quartile 1) - Indicates the journal ranks in the top 25% of journals in its field; ESWA holds Q1 status in Computer Science
