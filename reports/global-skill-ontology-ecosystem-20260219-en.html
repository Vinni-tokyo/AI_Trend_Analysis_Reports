Key Terminology

Skills Ontology -- A structured framework that defines the semantic relationships among skills, occupations, and tasks. Unlike a taxonomy, an ontology can express similarity, adjacency, and dependency between skills.
Skills Taxonomy -- A hierarchical classification system for skills. For example, "Programming > Python > Data Analysis" follows a parent-child structure. Simpler than an ontology but unable to capture cross-cutting relationships between skills.
Talent Intelligence -- Technology that leverages AI to analyze employees' skills, experience, and potential, supporting decision-making in hiring, deployment, and development (e.g., Eightfold AI, Beamery).
Skills Gap Analysis -- The process of identifying the difference between an organization's existing skills and the skills required to achieve its business objectives. Increasingly automated through skill ontology platforms.
Skills-Based Organization (SBO) -- An organizational model that centers talent deployment, evaluation, and career development on individual skills rather than traditional job descriptions. Championed by Deloitte and Gartner as the next-generation talent strategy.
