Key Terminology

SLM (Small Language Model) - Language models typically with 1-14B parameters. They require fewer computing resources than LLMs and excel when fine-tuned for specific domains, delivering specialized performance at a fraction of the cost.
On-Premise Deployment - Running AI models on organization-internal servers rather than external clouds. Data never leaves the organization, making it easier to comply with regulations like HIPAA and GDPR.
RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) - A technique where AI models search and reference relevant documents before generating responses. When combined with SLMs, even compact models can deliver accurate, domain-knowledge-based answers.
MoE (Mixture of Experts) - An architecture that activates only a subset of total parameters during inference. Like Qwen3-30B-A3B, which activates only 3B of its 30B total parameters, achieving large-model performance with compact resources.
Fine-Tuning - The process of additionally training a pre-trained general-purpose model on domain-specific data (e.g., medical records, legal documents) to impart specialized expertise.
FHIR (Fast Healthcare Interoperability Resources) - An international standard for healthcare information exchange. SLM-based medical agents leverage FHIR resources to perform tasks like ICD-10 code assignment.
On-Device AI - Running AI models directly on personal devices such as smartphones, laptops, and IoT devices rather than cloud servers. Provides instant responses without network latency and ensures personal data never leaves the device.
