1. Company Overview

Learning Economy Foundation (LEF) is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit founded in 2018, developing LearnCard, an open-source digital wallet platform. LearnCard serves as a "Lifelong Learning Passport" that empowers learners to collect, own, and share their skills and experiences.

Organization Information

  • Headquarters: Nashville, Tennessee, USA
  • Organization Type: 501(c)(3) Nonprofit
  • Employees: 11-50
  • Founders: Amy Purifoy, Ana Rold, Chris Purifoy, Jackson Smith
  • Websites: learncard.com, learningeconomy.io

Core Mission

  • Accelerate 21st-century education & workplace infrastructure
  • Address inequity with student/employee-centric models
  • Connect fragmented ecosystem into unified innovation hub
  • Promote individual agency, equity, and economic mobility
  • Build 22nd-century Web3-aligned infrastructure

2. Core Technology & Architecture

2.1 Open Source SDK & Plugin System

LearnCard is a robust open-source SDK (Software Development Kit) with both frontend and backend components. The plugin-based approach provides infinite flexibility and adaptability.

Supported Platforms

Web iOS Android macOS (M1+) visionOS

2.2 Interoperability & Standards Compliance

Standard Description Support Status
W3C Verifiable Credentials (VCs) Standard for secure, tamper-evident, portable digital records Full Support
W3C Decentralized Identifiers (DIDs) User-controlled globally unique identifiers Full Support
Open Badges 3.0 Latest 1EdTech Open Badges standard Full Support
VC-API Standard protocol for credential issuance/verification between wallets and servers Full Support
CTDL (Credential Transparency Description Language) Credential Engine's standard schema for skills and credentials Integrated

Blockchain Agnostic: LearnCard is not locked into any specific blockchain or database. The plugin approach enables flexible integration with various systems.

3. Skills Recommendation System & AI Features

Skills Bridge - Skills Connection Technology

Skills Bridge builds on the work of the Open Skills Network (OSN) to link skills text into a decentralized network. Similar to how HTTP linked text together into a navigable web of information, Skills Bridge builds skills into a connected network.

  • Rich Skill Descriptors (RSDs): Detailed skill descriptors for skills interoperability
  • CTDL Schema: Open standard schema for skills information exchange
  • Credential Engine Integration: Scaling LER ecosystems with Linked Open Data

3.1 LearnCloud Platform

LearnCloud is an open, API-driven platform that seamlessly integrates learning providers, tech systems, credentials, and AI-driven ecosystems. It enables rapid scaling and interoperability for the future of education and employment.

  • Connects digital wallets with verifiable credentials
  • Analytics dashboard integration
  • Connections across schools, employers, and global networks

3.2 LearningFREQUENCY Partnership

LEF partnered with LearningFREQUENCY alongside Gates Ventures, Animoca Brands, Open Campus, and Operation Kids. This helps learners understand their own unique learning and engagement preferences through metacognitive reflection. Currently deploying app-wide integration for LearnCard.

4. Key Deployment Cases

ScoutPass - World Scouting Movement

  • Partner: World Organization of the Scout Movement (WOSM)
  • Scale: 57 million Scouts across 172 countries
  • Achievement: 70,000+ badges issued in first week
  • 2024: 200,000+ verifiable credentials issued
  • Launch: December 2023 at World Scout Education Congress, Paris

Key Features:

Digital Badges Boosts (Social Badges) Troops (Org Management) Journeys (Learning Paths)

SuperSkills! - LEGO Foundation

  • Partner: LEGO Foundation
  • Target: Children ages 4-12
  • Concept: Learning Through Play
  • Launch: November 2021 closed beta

Skills Development Areas:

Cognitive Creative Social Emotional Physical

Driven by BrainPOP assessment research, analyzing learning and skills progressions for holistic development starting with creativity and cognitive thinking.

Privacy-First Design

The SuperSkills! project prioritizes protection of children and their data. Using a decentralized identity approach, none of the organizations involved are required to host users' data. All data ownership stays with the subject, and all data stewardship must be credibly portable.

Tech Stack: Custodial wallet (Torus) + Ceramic VC/storage tooling → Private, exportable, future-proof achievement records

8. Customer Satisfaction & Reviews

As a nonprofit open-source project, LearnCard has limited formal ratings on platforms like G2 or Gartner compared to commercial products. However, it has received global recognition:

Strengths

  • Fully open source ensuring transparency
  • Complete W3C, 1EdTech standards compliance
  • Global partnerships (LEGO, WOSM)
  • Privacy-first design
  • Free personal app
  • Blockchain agnostic flexibility
  • UNESCO Global Education Coalition member

Considerations

  • Limited commercial support structure
  • No formal user review platform ratings
  • Unclear enterprise SLAs
  • Limited Asia-specific deployments
  • Long-term sustainability questions (nonprofit model)

HundrED Global Recognition

LearnCard was selected as a global education innovation by HundrED, a nonprofit organization that discovers impactful education innovations worldwide. This recognizes its innovation and impact in the education sector.

11. Conclusions & Recommendations

Best Suited For

  • Organizations seeking open-source solutions
  • Educational institutions prioritizing privacy
  • Developers interested in Web3/decentralized systems
  • Nonprofit/public sector organizations
  • Youth organizations like Scouts
  • Institutions requiring global standards compliance

Consider Alternatives If

  • Immediate enterprise-ready solution needed
  • Commercial SLA and support contracts required
  • Immediate integration with existing HR/LMS needed
  • Large-scale enterprise deployment experience required

Final Assessment: LearnCard is the most innovative open-source solution in education and employment credentialing. It has built trust through its nonprofit mission and global partnerships (LEGO, WOSM, UNESCO). In Asia, it's expanding through AP Co-Lab and ScoutPass, making it particularly suitable for organizations that prioritize privacy protection and learner agency.
