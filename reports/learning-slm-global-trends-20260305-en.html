Key Terminology

Small Language Model (SLM) - A language model with 1 billion to 10 billion parameters. Lighter than Large Language Models (LLMs), SLMs can run directly on smartphones and laptops. Examples: Phi-4-mini, Gemma 3n, Llama 3.2
On-Device AI - Running AI directly on the user's device (smartphone, tablet, laptop) rather than on cloud servers. Functions without internet connectivity and offers enhanced data privacy
Fine-Tuning - The process of further training a pre-trained AI model with domain-specific data (e.g., math education, science experiments) to increase its expertise in that field
LoRA/QLoRA - Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning (PEFT) techniques that update only a tiny fraction of a model's total parameters, enabling fine-tuning with minimal GPU memory. Well-suited for educational institutions with limited computing resources
Multimodal - An AI model's ability to process multiple input types simultaneously — not just text, but also images, audio, and video. Essential for handling diverse learning materials in classroom settings
RAG (Retrieval Augmented Generation) - A technique where AI models search external databases or documents when generating responses. Enables accurate answers based on textbooks and learning resources
Edge Computing - Processing data near the user (on the device itself or local servers) rather than in central cloud servers. The primary deployment environment for SLMs
