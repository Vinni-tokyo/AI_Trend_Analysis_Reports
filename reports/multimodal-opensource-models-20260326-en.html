Key Terminology

Vision-Language Model (VLM) — An AI model that simultaneously understands and generates both images and text. Example: describing an image or extracting data from a document photo.
LoRA (Low-Rank Adaptation) — A technique for fine-tuning only a small subset of parameters rather than retraining an entire large model. Significantly reduces GPU costs while enabling customization.
Quantization — A technique that reduces a model's numerical precision (e.g., FP16 to INT4) to lower memory usage and compute costs. Quality loss is minimal.
MoE (Mixture of Experts) — An architecture that activates only a subset of "expert" modules from the total parameters. Maintains the performance of large-scale models while reducing execution costs (e.g., 22B active out of 235B total).
Diffusion Model — A method that progressively generates images or video from noise. The dominant architecture behind current image/video generation models such as FLUX, SDXL, and Wan.
3D VAE (Variational Autoencoder) — An encoder-decoder structure that transforms spatiotemporal video information into a compressed latent space. A core component of video models like HunyuanVideo.
ComfyUI — A node-based visual workflow tool. Build image/video generation pipelines via drag-and-drop. Popular among both developers and artists.
