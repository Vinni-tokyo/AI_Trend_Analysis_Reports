4. Global Skill Taxonomy Comparison: Lightcast, ESCO, and O*NET in Numbers

Current State: Skill taxonomies linked to Open Badges vary significantly by platform. Lightcast aggregates 32,000+ skills from over 40,000 data sources, while LinkedIn maintains 36,000+ skills derived from real-time job postings. The EU's ESCO provides 13,890 skills in 27 languages, and the US O*NET covers 2,400+ skills across 1,016 occupations.

📖 Key Terminology

Open Badge: A digital credential standard established by 1EdTech (formerly IMS Global). Metadata including issuer, criteria, and issue date is embedded within the badge image.
Micro-credential: A small-unit credential verifying a specific skill or competency. Faster and less costly to obtain than a traditional degree.
Skills Taxonomy: A hierarchical classification system for job skills. Prominent examples include Lightcast, O*NET, and ESCO.
Open Badges 3.0: The latest standard based on W3C Verifiable Credentials. Supports blockchain-agnostic verification and digital wallet storage.
Skills-Based Hiring: A recruitment approach that uses verified skill badges rather than academic degrees as primary criteria. IBM, Google, and Microsoft are early adopters.
Verifiable Credential (VC): A W3C-standard digital credential that can be stored in a personal wallet and selectively disclosed to verifiers.
