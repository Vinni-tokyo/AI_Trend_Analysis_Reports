Personalized AI Agents:
Global Trends and Japan/Korea Business Model Deep Dive

From foundation model memory to super-app integration to enterprise platforms
Published: May 26, 2026 | Standard Report (5–8 pages) | Report ID: TR-20260526-002

Top 5 Trends

1. Memory-Driven Personalization Becomes a Default Feature

Current state: In March 2026, Anthropic rolled out automatic Chat Memory to all Claude plans, synthesizing conversations every 24 hours to carry context forward. OpenAI's ChatGPT now automatically stores 40–80 facts about a typical monthly user, and Google launched Gemini "Personal Intelligence" for AI Pro/Ultra subscribers in January 2026. Then in March 2026, Gemini added the ability to import memory from both ChatGPT and Claude — opening up cross-platform portability that previously did not exist.

Vendor Memory Strategies

• ChatGPT: Builds a persistent user profile (preferences, work, relationships)
• Claude: All plans free (March 2026), 24-hour automatic synthesis
• Gemini: "Personal Intelligence" tied to Gmail, Calendar, and Drive

Business Impact

• Consumer demand: 87% value brands that recognize their history
• Revenue lift: Companies using AI personalization report 5–8% revenue gains
• Competitive shift: Portability tools signal a move away from lock-in

Outlook: By H2 2026, "memory" is likely to be treated as a personal data asset, with users carrying a "memory passport" across multiple AI services. In Japan and Korea, the contest over "who holds the user's memory" will intensify between foreign platforms (Google, OpenAI) and local super-apps (Naver, Rakuten, Kakao).

2. Agentic Commerce: From Search to Checkout, Executed Autonomously

Current state: In January 2026, Rakuten integrated agentic AI ("Rakuten AI") into the Rakuten Ichiba mobile app, enabling a "discovery shopping" experience where users describe budget, purpose, and use case via text, voice, or images and the agent surfaces and recommends optimized products. Korea's Naver launched a beta Shopping AI Agent on Naver Plus Store in February 2026 — parsing complex queries and purchase histories to deliver hyper-personalized recommendations. Naver leadership has labeled 2026 the "first year of full-scale AI monetization."

Rakuten AI Architecture

• Conversational shopping: Natural language input like "carry-on bag under ¥30,000"
• Credit-card data: Recommendations informed by Rakuten Card statement history
• Cross-service integration: Commerce, fintech, travel, mobile
• B2B offering: "Rakuten AI for Business" at ¥1,100/user/month

Naver Agent N Architecture

• Unified personal agent: Spans search, shopping, and finance
• Naver Plus tie-in: Linked to membership benefits and N Delivery
• Revenue contribution: AI surpassed 50% of ad-revenue growth in 2026
• GPU investment: KRW 1 trillion (~$691M) committed in 2026 alone

🔗 Related Links: Rakuten AI | Naver Plus Store | Rakuten Ichiba AI Press Release
📊 Credibility: 9/10
Strengths: Sourced from Rakuten official press releases and Seoul Economic Daily

Outlook: In H2 2026, "agentic checkout" — where AI orchestrates discovery, comparison, payment, and post-purchase support — is set to become mainstream. Expect a corresponding shift in marketplace revenue from advertising-driven models toward outcome-based commissions (per transaction or per resolution).

3. Super-App × Personal AI: A Distinctly Japan/Korea Integration Stack

Current state: The super-app culture unique to Japan and Korea is producing a "context-rich" personalization market that differs from the ChatGPT/Claude/Gemini stack. In January 2026, Kakao began rolling out hyper-personalized AI built on its on-device Kanana model, with "Kakao Tools" inside ChatGPT for Kakao that let users invoke KakaoTalk Gift, KakaoMap, and Kakao T directly. Rakuten, meanwhile, unveiled its cross-domain "Agentic Ecosystem" vision in July 2025, spanning shopping, fintech, travel, education, work, wellness, and entertainment.

Kakao Kanana Strategy

• On-device processing: Privacy-preserving by design
• KakaoTalk integration: Agent-based Kanana planned for H1 2026
• Kakao Tools: Direct invocation of national-scale services through ChatGPT for Kakao
• Next-gen: Kanana 2.5 push toward a full agent platform

Rakuten Ecosystem Strategy

• Rakuten AI 3.0: Released March 2026, Japan's largest domestic high-performance model (GENIAC project)
• Cross-service memory: Unified data across commerce, card, and travel
• Japanese context fluency: Optimized for local language and culture
• Merchant tools: RMS AI Assistant suite for sellers

4. The Enterprise Play: SoftBank's "Cristal Intelligence" Model

Current state: In November 2025, SoftBank and OpenAI formed SB OAI Japan (50% C Holdings, 50% OpenAI) to deliver "Cristal Intelligence" — an enterprise AI package combining OpenAI's frontier models with tailored systems integration — exclusively in Japan starting in 2026. SoftBank Corp. itself plans to spend $3 billion annually deploying Cristal across its own operations, targeting automation of more than 100 million workflows. In February 2026, the partnership announced acceleration via OpenAI's enterprise platform "Frontier."

Cristal Intelligence Structure

• Packaged solution: OpenAI's latest models bundled with SI services
• Multi-agent orchestration: Agents collaborate to complete business tasks
• Core use cases: Financial reporting, document drafting, customer service automation
• Japan exclusivity: Sold through SB OAI Japan from 2026

Market Sizing and Competitors

• Japan AI agent market: 49.9% CAGR (2026–2033)
• Korea market: Projected to reach $7.65B by 2033
• Competitive layer: Samsung and LG are betting on hosting, orchestration, and governance infrastructure
• Penetration: 40% of enterprise apps will embed task-specific AI agents by end-2026

5. Vertical AI Agents Eat Into SaaS

Current state: Andreessen Horowitz's 2025 report "AI Eats Vertical SaaS" estimates that 30–40% of the global $450B vertical SaaS market will be reshaped by AI agents between 2026 and 2028. Gartner predicts that 80% of enterprises will adopt vertical AI agents by 2026. In healthcare, Hippocratic AI (valued at $1.6B in 2025) targets non-diagnostic interactions — pre-op education, chronic care, medication reminders — while Suki AI generates clinical notes in real time from physician-patient conversations.

Vertical Economics

• Pricing premium: Healthcare/legal/finance agents command higher prices than horizontal tools
• Retention edge: Specialized agents see 3–5× higher retention
• Pricing shift: From "license seats" to "tickets resolved"
• Moats: Compliance and data integration are natural barriers to entry

Vertical Moves in Japan/Korea

• Finance: Rakuten Card launched an AI agent inside Rakuten Card Lite (Feb 2026)
• Travel: Rakuten Travel enhanced its hotel recommendation agent (April 2026)
• Korea B2B: Naver is rolling out vertical agents across search, finance, and health by year-end
• Regulation: Korea's Digital Asset Basic Act (June 2025) imposes new notification duties on B2C AI

Competitive Landscape and Market Positioning

4-Tier Market Structure

The personalized AI agent market splits cleanly into four tiers — from global foundation models to regional super-apps, enterprise platforms, and vertical specialists. What makes Japan and Korea distinctive is that Tier 2 (super-apps) is far stronger here than in any other region.

Tier Representative Players Revenue Model Primary Target Regional Strength
Tier 1: Global Foundation Models with Memory ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini Monthly subscription ($20–$200) Individual knowledge workers Global
Tier 2: Japan/Korea Super-App Integration Rakuten AI, Naver Agent N, Kakao Kanana Commission + ads + B2B SaaS Consumers and SMB merchants Japan / Korea
Tier 3: Enterprise AI Platforms SB OAI Japan (Cristal), Samsung SDS, LG CNS Annual contract + usage-based Large enterprises and public sector Japan / Korea (exclusive distribution)
Tier 4: Vertical Specialist Agents Hippocratic AI, Suki AI, Sierra (CS) Outcome-based (per resolution) Industry-specific enterprises US-led; Japan/Korea are adopters

Five Key Market Shifts

  • Memory portability: From March 2026, Gemini, Claude, and ChatGPT support cross-platform memory transfer — eroding traditional lock-in
  • Regional infrastructure investment: Naver (KRW 1T) and SoftBank ($3B/year) are anchoring local AI build-outs
  • Revenue model diversification: Subscriptions giving way to commission, outcome-based, and hybrid pricing
  • Regulation: Japan's amended Payment Services Act (June 2025) and Korea's Digital Asset Basic Act add new obligations for B2C AI
  • APAC ascent: The US still holds 40.1% revenue share, but APAC (including Japan/Korea) leads in CAGR

Application Ideas

Three approaches observed in the market for organizations evaluating personalized AI agents.

1. Memory-Connected Customer Experiences — Use ChatGPT/Claude/Gemini memory APIs or MCP connections to deliver service interactions that build on each user's prior conversations and purchase history (e.g., personalized onboarding flows).

2. Super-App API Integration — In Japan, use Rakuten AI for Business (¥1,100/month) or Rakuten's RMS AI Assistant. In Korea, use the Kakao Tools API to make your service callable from the national super-app's agent surface.

3. Vertical Specialist PoC — Focus on a regulated, knowledge-intensive workflow inside your organization and run a joint pilot with SB OAI Japan Cristal, Samsung SDS, or a vertical specialist (Hippocratic AI, Suki AI, etc.).

Key Terminology

Personalized AI Agent — An AI system that remembers individual preferences, history, and context to act autonomously on a user's behalf.
Agentic Commerce — Commerce in which AI agents handle the full loop from product search to purchase and payment.
Super-App — An app that bundles messaging, commerce, payments, maps, and more — characteristic of Japan/Korea/Southeast Asia (e.g., KakaoTalk, LINE, Rakuten).
Memory Portability — The ability to transfer personal profile and history data between AI services.
Vertical AI Agent — An AI agent purpose-built for a specific industry (healthcare, legal, finance) with embedded domain knowledge and compliance.
Outcome-Based Pricing — A pricing model where customers pay per resolution, per action, or per result rather than per seat.
Cristal Intelligence — The enterprise AI package delivered by SB OAI Japan, the SoftBank–OpenAI joint venture.
