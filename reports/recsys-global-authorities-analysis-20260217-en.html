Key Terminology

Collaborative Filtering - A technique that recommends items based on past behavior data from users with similar tastes. Operates on the principle of "recommending what similar users have liked"
Matrix Factorization - A mathematical technique that decomposes a large user-item rating matrix into low-dimensional latent factors to predict empty cells (unrated items)
GNN (Graph Neural Network) - A deep learning model that learns patterns from graph-structured data (nodes and edges). In recommender systems, user-item interactions are modeled as graphs
Knowledge Graph - A database structuring real-world entities (people, places, concepts) and their relationships in graph form. Google Knowledge Graph and AMiner OAG are representative examples
Explainable Recommendation - A recommender system that explains "why this was recommended" in a human-understandable format. Essential for compliance with regulations like the EU AI Act
NCF (Neural Collaborative Filtering) - A framework proposed by Xiangnan He that replaces traditional inner product-based collaborative filtering with neural networks to learn non-linear user-item interactions
LightGCN - A lightweight graph convolution network proposed by He that removes feature transformation and nonlinear activation from standard GCN, retaining only neighborhood aggregation to improve both recommendation performance and efficiency
