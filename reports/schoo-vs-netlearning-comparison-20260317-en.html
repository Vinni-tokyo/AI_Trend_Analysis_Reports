Key Terminology

Open Badge 3.0 - The latest version of the Open Badges standard maintained by 1EdTech, enabling verifiable, portable digital credentials. Badges contain metadata about the issuer, criteria, and evidence, and can be verified across platforms and borders
LMS (Learning Management System) - A software platform for administering, documenting, tracking, reporting, and delivering educational courses and training programs. NetLearning's Multiverse is an enterprise-grade LMS
SCORM (Sharable Content Object Reference Model) - A set of technical standards for e-learning software products, enabling interoperability between different LMS platforms and content
xAPI (Experience API) - A modern e-learning specification that allows learning content and systems to communicate about learner experiences, supporting more granular tracking than SCORM
TSE Growth Market - Tokyo Stock Exchange Growth Market, designed for high-growth companies. Schoo listed here in October 2024, signaling its transition from startup to publicly accountable enterprise
SaaS (Software as a Service) - A software distribution model where applications are hosted in the cloud and provided to customers on a subscription basis. Schoo's flat-rate per-user model is a classic SaaS approach
ARR (Annual Recurring Revenue) - A key SaaS metric representing the annualized value of recurring subscription revenue, used to measure predictable revenue growth
