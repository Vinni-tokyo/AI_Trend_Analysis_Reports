Key Terminology

Diffusion Transformer - An AI model architecture that progressively generates video from noise. The foundational technology that understands text prompts and converts them into high-quality video
4-Modality Input - The ability to simultaneously accept four types of data: text, image, audio, and video. First implemented by Seedance 2.0
Native Audio-Video Co-Generation - Technology that creates audio simultaneously during the video generation process. Unlike traditional methods that add audio after video creation, both are generated in a single pass
Elo Score - A relative rating score derived from the chess rating system. Used in the Artificial Analysis Video Arena to compare AI video model quality based on user voting
Text-to-Video - AI technology that automatically generates video from text descriptions alone. Converts prompts like "a dog running on a beach" into actual video footage
Phoneme-Level Lip Sync - Technology that synchronizes character lip movements to the smallest units of pronunciation (phonemes). Accurately reflects the pronunciation characteristics of each language when supporting multilingual output
