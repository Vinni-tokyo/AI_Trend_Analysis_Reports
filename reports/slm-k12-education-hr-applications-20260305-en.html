Key Terminology

MatFormer (Matryoshka Transformer) - A transformer architecture where smaller models are nested inside larger ones, like Russian Matryoshka dolls. The core technology enabling Gemma 3n to use only 2-4B levels of memory despite having 8B parameters
On-Device AI - Running AI directly on user devices (Chromebooks, tablets, smartphones) rather than cloud servers. Advantageous for privacy as data never leaves the device
FERPA (Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act) - U.S. federal law regulating access to and disclosure of student educational records. The legal standard for student data protection when schools use AI tools
COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) - U.S. federal law regulating online collection of personal information from children under 13. Protection scope expanded by FTC rule amendments in January 2025
LoRA/QLoRA (Low-Rank Adaptation / Quantized Low-Rank Adaptation) - Techniques for customizing models to specific domains by training only small adapters instead of the entire model. Enables fine-tuning of 3B models on a single GPU
Bias Audit - An independent process verifying whether AI hiring tools produce discriminatory results based on protected characteristics such as gender or race. Mandated annually in NYC
SFT (Supervised Fine-Tuning) - Additional model training using human-created input-output pair datasets. Both SmolLM3 and Llama 3.2 can be specialized for HR domains through SFT
