1. Company Overview

Gobekli is a startup founded in 2020 developing TalentPass, a user-friendly, standards-compliant digital wallet for Learning and Employment Records (LER). The company collaborates with the Experience You Project supported by U.S. Chamber of Commerce Foundation and Education Design Lab.

Company Information

  • Founded: 2020
  • Type: Startup (Private)
  • Funding: Under $40,000
  • Products: TalentPass, TalentSync
  • HQ: United States

Core Technology

2. Business Model

TalentPass is free for individuals, while organizations pay annual per-user fees for Passport Pages hosting.

3. AI Recommendation System

Pythia is TalentPass's AI guide that captures user talent information and transforms it into a visual Talent Tree.

Pythia AI Guide

Through conversation, Pythia captures all talent information - from formal credentials and job skills to abilities developed as family member, volunteer, artist, athlete, creator, leader, or learner. Data becomes trusted, portable, and beautifully visual through Talent Tree.

8. Conclusion & Recommendations

Overall Assessment

TalentPass is an innovative digital wallet solution based on learner-centered data ownership and LER standards. University of Phoenix pilot provides technical validation, but limited funding and early stage mean large-scale adoption requires time. Suitable for institutions interested in learner-owned data ecosystems.

Best Suited For

  • Institutions adopting LER standards
  • Organizations prioritizing learner data ownership
  • Universities interested in innovative pilots
  • Individual learners managing portfolios

Consider Alternatives

  • Need immediate large-scale deployment
  • Require stable enterprise solutions
  • Need proven large user base
  • Require Asia region support
