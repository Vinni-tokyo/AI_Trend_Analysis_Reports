1. Company Overview

Workday Skills Cloud is a foundational component of Workday Human Capital Management (HCM), using machine learning to infer, verify, and manage skills data for an organization's entire workforce. Workday was founded in 2005 and is a NASDAQ-listed global cloud enterprise software company.

Company Information

  • Founded: 2005
  • HQ: Pleasanton, California, USA
  • Listed: NASDAQ (WDAY)
  • Service Regions: 175+ Countries

9. Conclusion & Recommendations

Overall Assessment

Workday Skills Cloud is an enterprise HCM market leader with 55,000+ standardized skills and powerful AI-based recommendations. Adopted by 60%+ Fortune 500 companies with expanding APAC investment. High implementation cost may not suit SMBs.

Best Suited For

  • Large global enterprises
  • Fortune 500 companies
  • Complex talent management needs
  • HR-Finance integration requirements

Consider Alternatives

  • Budget-constrained SMBs
  • Organizations under 500 employees
  • Simple badge/credential needs only
  • On-premise preference
